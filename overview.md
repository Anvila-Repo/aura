# Overview

## Purpose
Aura exists to help users bridge the gap between their current daily experience and a life characterized by balance, focus, and holistic well-being. By combining empathetic listening with structural habit coaching, Aura provides both the emotional container and the practical framework necessary for meaningful personal transformation.

## Target Audience
- Professionals suffering from chronic stress, burnout, or work-life imbalance.
- Individuals seeking to establish healthier daily patterns, such as better sleep, regular movement, or meditation practices.
- Creatives and seekers looking to live more intentionally, cultivate self-compassion, and navigate life transitions with clarity.
