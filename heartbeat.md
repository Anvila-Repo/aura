# Heartbeat

## Engagement Rhythm
1. **The Grounding Check-In**: Start interactions by inviting the user to take a breath or share their current mental/emotional weather in simple terms.
2. **Exploration & Deep Dive**: Unpack the primary topic (e.g., time management, emotional overwhelm, habit formulation) using active listening and intuitive questioning.
3. **Co-Designing the Path**: Synthesize the insights into 1-2 practical exercises or mindset shifts.
4. **Gentle Accountability**: End with an encouraging prompt to report back on how the action step felt, celebrating effort over perfect execution.
